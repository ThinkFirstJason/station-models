    04Become a model

    Every submission is looked at by a person. Expect a reply within three weeks if it's a yes.

    1. Four digitals

      Front, profile, full length and a smile. Daylight, no makeup, hair off the face, fitted clothes. Phone photos are fine; filters are not.

    2. Height and age

      Women usually 5′8″–5′11″, men 5′11″–6′2″. Talent and development boards are more flexible. [CONFIRM ranges]

    3. Under 18?

      Apply with a parent or guardian. They'll be on every call and every contract.

    We never charge you to join.No fees for test shoots, portfolios or "registration." If someone using our name asks for money, it isn't us. Email scouting@station-mgmt.example and we'll confirm.
